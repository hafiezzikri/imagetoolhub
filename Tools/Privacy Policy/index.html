This policy explains our data practices. Please read it carefully to understand how information may be handled when you use ImageToolsHub.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ImageToolsHub. We value your privacy and aim to handle information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you visit or use our website and its image-processing tools.

By using ImageToolsHub, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will seek that consent separately.

2. Information We Collect

You do not need to create an account to use the basic features of ImageToolsHub. However, certain information may be processed when you visit the website.

Technical information

Our hosting provider and other services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, requested pages, and server logs.

Usage information

If analytics tools are enabled, information about page visits, tool usage, traffic sources, and interactions may be collected to help us understand website performance.

Information you provide

If you contact us, we may process the email address and information you include in your message so that we can respond.

3. Image Processing and Uploaded Files

ImageToolsHub provides tools for compressing, resizing, cropping, converting, and otherwise processing images.

Some tools may process images directly in your browser. When this happens, the image is processed on your device by that tool rather than being uploaded to our server by the tool.

Processing methods may vary by feature. If a tool uploads files to a server or uses an external service, the relevant information should be disclosed before you use that feature.

Please avoid using images containing confidential or sensitive information unless you understand how the specific tool processes them.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ImageToolsHub or its service providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, and similar technologies for website functionality, security, analytics, and advertising.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember certain website preferences.
  • Understand website traffic and performance.
  • Measure advertising performance.
  • Help detect invalid activity and prevent fraud.
  • Support advertising personalization where permitted.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect certain features. Where legally required, we will provide appropriate consent choices before using non-essential tracking technologies.

5. Third-Party Advertising

ImageToolsHub may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Monetag.

Advertising providers may process information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, cookie identifiers, and advertising interactions to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, or personalize advertisements where applicable.

The information processed and the technologies used depend on the advertising services enabled on our website and your privacy choices.

To learn more about Monetag's data practices, review its Privacy Policy .

We will update this section if our advertising providers or practices change.

6. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may rely on third-party services for hosting, content delivery, analytics, security, advertising, or other functions.

External websites linked from ImageToolsHub operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of independent websites or services.

We encourage you to review the applicable privacy policies of those providers.

7. How We Use Information

Depending on the services enabled, information may be processed to:

  • Operate and maintain ImageToolsHub.
  • Provide and improve website features.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to user inquiries.
  • Understand website usage and performance.
  • Support advertising and measure ad performance.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We aim to limit processing to purposes that are appropriate and relevant to operating the website.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ between ImageToolsHub and third-party providers.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or to obtain information about how it is processed.

You may also have the right to object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or submit a complaint to a relevant authority, where applicable.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

10. Children's Privacy

ImageToolsHub is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in circumstances that violate applicable law, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, tools, service providers, advertising arrangements, or legal obligations change.

Any revised version will be published on this page. Please check the Last updated date at the top to see when this policy was most recently revised.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us.
